Section 04 · About

We don't pick sides. We pick votes.

We don't pick sides. We pick votes. Liar's Ledger does not editorialize, rank politicians, or add partisan framing. It shows how officials voted, linked back to congress.gov, house.gov, and senate.gov.

The free Ledger is non-partisan by design. It shows how officials voted, sponsored bills, cosponsored bills, roll-call votes, interest group ratings all linked directly back to congress.gov and VoteSmart. No commentary. No ranking. No spin. If it's not in the official record, it's not in the Ledger.

The extension is open source so anyone can audit detection rules, source matching, and record delivery. If it is not in the official record, it is not in the ledger.
